## What this covers

This policy explains what Rovie collects when you create an account, top up a
balance and make calls through the gateway, and what we do with it. It covers
`rovie.africa` and the API at `api.rovie.africa`..

It does **not** cover what the model providers do with a prompt once we route
it to them. That is a separate question, and it is answered on the
[Data policy](/data-policy) page.

## What we collect

**When you register.** An email address or phone number, and your country. The
country is what lets us quote prices in your currency; the contact detail is
how we reach you about your account. We do not ask for a name, a card, or an
address to sign up.

**Your API key.** We store a hash of every key we issue so we can recognise it
on a request. The key itself is shown to you once, at creation, and we cannot
retrieve it afterwards.

**Your balance and its history.** Every top-up and every charge against your
key, so the number we show you can be reconciled.

**Usage records.** For each request: which model, how many tokens, what it
cost, and when. This is what bills you correctly and what the
[rankings](/rankings) are aggregated from. Rankings never identify an account.

**Payment details.** Handled by IvoryPay, not by us. We receive a payment
reference, an amount and a currency; we do not receive or store card numbers or
bank credentials.

**Approximate location.** The site asks a geolocation service for the country
your IP appears to be in, so it can guess your currency before you tell it. You
can override it at any time, and it is used for display only.

## What we do not collect

We do not run third-party advertising or analytics trackers on this site. We do
not sell personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

## Why we hold it

- To run your account and authenticate your requests.
- To bill accurately and let you check a balance.
- To detect abuse and protect the service.
- To meet the record-keeping obligations that come with handling payments.

## Who else sees it

- **Model providers** receive the contents of the requests we route to them.
  See [Data policy](/data-policy).
- **IvoryPay** processes top-ups and receives what a payment needs.
- **Infrastructure providers** host the services; they hold data at rest on our
  behalf and do not use it for their own purposes.

## Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask us to
close your account and delete it, subject to records we are required to keep.
Write to [hello@rovie.africa](mailto:hello@rovie.africa) and we will respond.

You can rotate or revoke an API key at any time without affecting the rest of
your account.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will say so on this page and date the
change. Continuing to use Rovie after a change means the updated policy
applies.
